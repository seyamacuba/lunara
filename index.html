Scientific scope & limitations

MODEL TRACEABILITY
Ephemeris / geometry

With positions.json: Sun and Earth vectors precomputed by SPICE in IAU_MOON. Linear interpolation is performed within dataset coverage; no extrapolation is applied beyond the available range.

Horizon / terrain

Without a profile: ideal tangential horizon. With CSV: local horizon-elevation mask by azimuth. LOLA DEM integration is still required for terrain-realistic occultation.

Communications

Visible Earth disk is a preliminary geometric filter. Antennas, RF power, frequency, polarization, ground stations, 3D terrain occultation and link budget are not modeled.

Power

Irradiance is projected onto a flat panel with configurable tilt, azimuth and efficiency. This is not a substitute for an electrical/thermal spacecraft power-system model.

Thermal environment

No falsely precise surface temperature is displayed. A thermal model needs thermal inertia, conductivity, emissivity, albedo, regolith properties and topography.

Data quality

Time resolution and coverage are determined by the loaded file. 3D models in this version are illustrative, not a certified topographic mesh.

IMPORTANT: demo data must not be used for mission decisions. Load a validated SPICE dataset and a LOLA-derived horizon profile for a more realistic assessment.
